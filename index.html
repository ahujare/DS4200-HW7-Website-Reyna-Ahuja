Hello! My name is Reyna, and I am a Behavioral Neuroscience student with a minor in Data Science. I am currently applying to medical school where I hope to stay in either Boston or New York City. In the future, I hope to work in underserved populations in the field of either addiction medicine or psychiatry. I currently work at Boston Healthcare for the Homeless Program which is where I first got exposed to vulnerable populations who often have substance use disorders or mental health problems. I became interested in how I can support these patients through harm reduction, or the reducing of negative consequences associated with drug use.

Harm reduction is often considered an intermediary step in between drug use and abstinence. By supporting patients with harm reduction instead of demanding abstinence from them, it allows for trust to build. This is essential to ensuring patients feel safe and comfortable in my care and are thus more successful in their recoveries. As a future physician, I want to be able to provide effective and sustainable care for my patients long-term. Working with underserved populations in addiction medicine and harm reduction will allow me to fulfill these goals as well as improve patient outcomes, while maintaining long-term relationships with them.
